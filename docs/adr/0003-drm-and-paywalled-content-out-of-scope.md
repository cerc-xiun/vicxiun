# DRM and paywalled content are permanently out of scope

A link to DRM-protected or paywalled content is refused with a named error and
a clear explanation, and the refusal is documented as a feature rather than
apologised for. Attempting such content would either end in a failure the tool
cannot honestly explain, or pull the user's credentials and a circumvention
into the project's scope; a refusal stated up front costs the user less time
than a failure discovered halfway through a Queue.

**_Rejected_**: using the user's own account to reach content they already have
access to. It is deferred rather than forbidden forever — but not in this
milestone, because it would make the tool's behaviour depend on a site's terms
and put credentials somewhere the project promised to keep them out of.
